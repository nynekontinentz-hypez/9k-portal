Most common repairs (virus removal, software issues, RAM upgrades, screen replacements for in-stock parts) are completed same day or next day. More complex repairs or those requiring part orders typically take 2–5 business days. We'll give you an honest timeline upfront before any work begins.
We charge a diagnostic fee for in-person assessments, but this fee is waived if you proceed with the repair. If we diagnose the issue and you decide not to repair, you pay only the diagnostic fee — no further charges. We'll always tell you what we found and what it'll cost before we do any paid work.
